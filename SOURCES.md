### Trusted forms (`de/frequency/trusted_forms.tsv`)

Derived data, our own: the forms of `de/frequency/forms.tsv` (count ≥ 16) that the lexicon lacks
but that are real German words, with their total count. Generated by `heinrich-lexicon trusted`
(Heinrich `docs/LEXICON_TOOLS.md`) only from our own lexicon, frequency list, name lists and the
spell checker; no third-party word list, dictionary or spell checker was used. Built into
`de-freq.pack` as a marking (Heinrich `docs/PACK_FORMAT.md` §5). State 2026-10-20: 6,128 forms. One form per line with its counts, its `classify` class and the
evidence that it is a word, so that a reviewer can check every entry. Every entry of the first
list was checked by the clean-room reviewer; the 315 forms it disputed are in
`de/frequency/trusted_excluded.tsv` (form and kind of objection only, no reference value) and are
never trusted.

## German lexicon (`de/lexicon/`)

Our own entries, written clean-room from general knowledge of German grammar and the official
spelling rules (no third-party lexicon, word list, Hunspell file, Wiktionary table or LanguageTool
file was consulted). Format: `Heinrich/docs/LEXICON_FORMAT.md`.

Work lists in `de/lexicon/worklists/` are derived only from our lexicon and our own frequency list
(`de/frequency/forms.tsv`) by `heinrich-lexicon next-batch`.

Lexeme classes in `de/lexicon/classes/` (time nouns, verbs of saying, directed motion verbs,
valency, perfect auxiliary by use, mass nouns) are our own lists, written clean-room from general knowledge of
German grammar; they contain only lemmas of our lexicon.

## Name lists (`de/names/`)

`given_names.tsv` (about 2,750 given names with gender m/f/u), `surnames.tsv` (about 2,500
common surnames in Germany, Austria and Switzerland and about 1,950 names of persons of the school
and general-education canon) and `places.tsv` (about 3,250 place names in their German form:
countries, states and cantons, cities, rivers, mountains, regions, islands, waters) are our own
data, written clean-room from general knowledge of names in German-speaking schools and families,
of the school canon (literature, philosophy, religion, music, art, science, history, politics)
and of school geography. Candidates were checked only against our own lexicon and our own
frequency list `de/frequency/forms.tsv` (2026-10-20). No third-party name list, gazetteer, register
statistic, directory or web source was consulted. Format and use: `de/names/README.md`.

## English name lists (`en/names/`)

`given_names.tsv` (about 2,800 given names with gender m/f/u), `surnames.tsv` (about 2,700
