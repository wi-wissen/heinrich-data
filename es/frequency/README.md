# Spanish word-form frequency list (`es/frequency/`)

Our own counts, made with the same method as `de/frequency/`, `en/frequency/` and `fr/frequency/` (scripts in
`tools/frequency/`, see `tools/frequency/README.md`). Texts were streamed and discarded; only token counts are
stored. No third-party word list, frequency list, lexicon or dictionary was used (no wordfreq, hermitdave,
CREA/CORPES lists of the RAE, Leipzig corpora, SUBTLEX-ESP, Hunspell/LibreOffice dictionaries, Wiktionary or
similar). Counted on 2026-10-21.

## Files
- `forms.tsv` - case-sensitive word forms with count >= 3, sorted by total count. Columns:
  `form`, `total`, `wiki`, `lit`, `gov`, `sentence_initial` - the same columns as German and French. There are
  no variety columns: Spanish is es-ES only for now (Heinrich `docs/SPANISH.md`); American varieties can be
  added later like `us`/`gb` in English.
- `STATS.md` - tokens per source, coverage curve, top 50 forms (generated by `merge.py`).
- `lit_books.tsv` - the Project Gutenberg books counted (id, persons as in the catalog, title, tokens).
- `lit_books_old.tsv` - Gutenberg books **not** counted because they are printed in the old orthography
  (see below), with their rate of old-orthography markers per 1000 tokens.

## Normalisation
- Token = run of Latin letters (accents, `ñ`, `ü` kept), optionally joined by single hyphens or apostrophes
  between letters; NFC; anything touching a digit is dropped, so numbers with a decimal comma (`3,5`), thousands
  points (`1.500`) and ordinals (`1.º`, `2.ª`) produce no token. Case is kept as in German, English and French
  (`El` and `el` are separate forms; `sentence_initial` counts how often a form opens a sentence).
- `¿ ¡ « » “ ”` and the dialogue dash `—` are punctuation, never part of a token (`¿Qué` -> `Qué`). A sentence
  may open with `¿` or `¡`, also after a quote or a dash (`—¿Vienes?`); the splitter knows this.
- **Apostrophes** `’ ʼ ‘ ‛ ´ \` ′` between letters are unified to `'` (`O'Donnell`); a trailing apostrophe is
  dropped. Hyphens U+2010/U+2011 become `-`. Spanish has no elision, so nothing is split off.
- Contractions `al`, `del` and enclitic pronouns (`dámelo`, `decírselo`) stay one token, as written.
- Sentence splitting knows Spanish abbreviations (`Sr.`, `Sra.`, `Srta.`, `Dña.`, `Excmo.`, `art.`, `núm.`,
  `pág.`, `etc.`, `ej.`, `Ud.`, `Vd.` ...); single letters (`D.`, `p. ej.`, `n.º`) do not end a sentence.

## Source specifics
- Wikipedia: wikitext cleaned like the other languages (templates, refs, tables, file and category lines,
  headings dropped; for Spanish also inline file links with several pipes and doubly escaped entities such as
  `&amp;nbsp;`); sections from `Referencias`, `Notas`, `Enlaces externos`, `Véase también`, `Bibliografía`,
  `Fuentes`, `Obras`, `Discografía`, `Filmografía` ... on are cut; `#REDIRECCIÓN` pages skipped. Spanish
  Wikipedia is pan-Hispanic: most of it is not es-ES and is counted without a marker.
- Gutenberg: PG header/footer stripped; every named author, editor and translator (illustrators ignored) died
  in 1954 or earlier; the first author was born in 1700 or later (no Golden Age spelling such as `dixo`,
  `assí`); at most 12 kept books per author; authors from Spain first (`CANON_ES` in `count_gutenberg.py`,
  bibliographic knowledge only), the rest in a fixed pseudo-random order. 93 of the 140 books (6,446,224
  tokens, 69 %) are by authors from Spain; 73 authors in all. In Spain authors who died before 7 December 1987 are protected
  for life + 80 years, so some of these works (authors who died 1946-1954) are still protected there; we keep
  only counts, under the text-and-data-mining exception.
- **Old orthography is filtered out.** Most Gutenberg editions are 19th-century prints with the accents that
  the RAE dropped in 1911 (`á`, `é`, `ó`, `ú` as words) and 1952 (`fué`, `dió`, `vió`, `fuí`, `pié`). For every book
  the rate of such markers (and of Golden Age forms `dixo`, `assí`, `quando`, `qual`, `mesmo`; list `OLD_ES`) per
  1000 tokens is computed; books with a rate of 0.2 or more are not counted (their counts were kept outside the
  repository for inspection only). Most old prints have rates of 10-31 (`á` everywhere); 103 books have 1-5
  (prints of 1911-1952 with `fué`, `dió` but without `á`), 18 have 0.2-1; 130 of the 140 kept books are below
  0.05. 378 books (26,297,293 tokens) were excluded and are listed in `lit_books_old.tsv`. Residual old forms
  in the kept books and in quotations elsewhere stay as they are (`fué` 23, `á` 510 in `forms.tsv`; most `á`
  are the letter name or foreign words in Wikipedia).
- BOE: texts from the BOE open data API (`legislacion-consolidada`), the current (last) version of every
  preamble and precept block; headings, article titles, footnotes, signatures and tables skipped. Norms of
  ámbito Estatal with `vigencia_agotada = N` only. The 3,954 norms are in 10 count files by a hash of their id
  (all 10 are counted). No regional (autonómico) laws: their language may follow regional usage, and some are
  translations.
- Congreso: the full text of each Diario de Sesiones from congreso.es (HTML). Only speech paragraphs from the
  first speaker on: the summary and order of business, headings in capitals, speaker names
  (`El señor PRESIDENTE:`), stage directions in parentheses (`(Aplausos)`, `(Rumores)`), vote results and page
  markers are skipped; a paragraph cut by a page break is joined. Legislature XV (from 2023) is not used because
  the chamber then admitted Catalan, Galician and Basque and the record carries those speeches unmarked; in
  X-XIV a few greetings in those languages occur, and paragraphs that read as Catalan, Galician or Basque (more
  of our own non-Spanish function words than Spanish ones, `foreign()` in `count_gov_es.py`) are dropped.
  Numbers that are not online (legislature X starts at about no. 50) are skipped.
- The Senado was not counted: senado.es refuses connections from the counting environment (HTTP 403). Its
  Diario de Sesiones is open data under CC BY 4.0 (`/SOURCES.md`) and can be added later.

## Known limitations
- Literature is small (7 % of the tokens; French has 27 %) because only 140 public-domain books in modern
  orthography are on Gutenberg; the per-source columns allow weighting.
- Wikipedia mixes all varieties of Spanish (`computadora`/`ordenador`, `carro`/`coche`); the `gov` column is
  es-ES only and can be used to tell them apart.
- The traditional accented forms `sólo`, `éste` (S-D9) are counted as written (`solo` 77,772 / `sólo` 23,244).
- List markers `a)`, `b)`, `i)` in laws are counted as one-letter tokens (also as sentence-initial).
- Foreign words and names are counted as they are; the tail is noisy.
