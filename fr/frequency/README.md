# French word-form frequency list (`fr/frequency/`)

Our own counts, made with the same method as `de/frequency/` and `en/frequency/` (scripts in
`tools/frequency/`, see `tools/frequency/README.md`). Texts were streamed and discarded; only token
counts are stored. No third-party word list, frequency list, lexicon or dictionary was used (no wordfreq,
hermitdave, Lexique, Leipzig corpora, Morphalou, Lefff, Wiktionnaire or similar). Counted on 2026-10-20.

## Files
- `forms.tsv` - case-sensitive word forms with count >= 3, sorted by total count. Columns:
  `form`, `total`, `wiki`, `lit`, `gov`, `sentence_initial` - the same columns as German. There are no
  variety columns: French is fr-FR only for now (Heinrich `docs/FRENCH.md` F-D2); Belgian, Swiss and
  Canadian columns can be added later like `us`/`gb` in English.
- `STATS.md` - tokens per source, coverage curve, top 50 forms (generated by `merge.py`).
- `lit_books.tsv` - the Project Gutenberg books counted (id, persons as in the catalog, title, tokens).

## Normalisation
- Token = run of Latin letters (accents, `œ`, `æ` kept), optionally joined by single hyphens or apostrophes
  between letters; NFC; anything touching a digit is dropped. Case is kept as in German and English
  (`Le` and `le` are separate forms; `sentence_initial` counts how often a form opens a sentence).
- **Elision is split off**: `l' d' j' m' n' s' t' c' qu' lorsqu' puisqu' jusqu' quoiqu'` (any case) become
  their own token, apostrophe included: `l'homme` -> `l'` + `homme`, `L'État` -> `L'` + `État`,
  `qu'il` -> `qu'` + `il`, `jusqu'aujourd'hui` -> `jusqu'` + `aujourd'hui`. Inner apostrophes of other
  words stay (`aujourd'hui`, `presqu'île`, `quelqu'un`, `prud'homme`, old `grand'mère`); `c'est-à-dire` is
  kept whole. A space after the clitic (`l' homme`, old prints) is closed up first.
- **Apostrophes** `’ ʼ ‘ ‛ ´ \` ′` between letters are unified to `'`; hyphens U+2010/U+2011 to `-`.
- Hyphenated forms stay one token (`peut-être`, `a-t-il`, `est-ce`, `vingt-trois`, `Saint-Étienne`).
- Sentence splitting knows French abbreviations (`MM.`, `Mme`, `Mlle`, `Mgr`, `art.`, `chap.`, `cf.`, `p.` ...)
  and guillemets with (narrow) no-break spaces (`? » Il`, `. « Oui`).

## Source specifics
- Wikipedia: wikitext cleaned like German/English (templates, refs, tables, file and category lines,
  headings dropped); sections from `Notes et références`, `Références`, `Liens externes`, `Voir aussi`,
  `Bibliographie`, `Annexes`, `Articles connexes` ... on are cut.
- Gutenberg: PG header/footer stripped; every named author, editor and translator (illustrators ignored)
  died in 1954 or earlier (works of authors who died "mort pour la France" may still be protected in
  France for some years; we keep only counts, under the text-and-data-mining exception); the first author
  was born in 1600 or later (no Middle French such as Rabelais or Montaigne); at most 12 books per author;
  canonical authors first (`CANON_FR` in `count_gutenberg.py`), the rest in a fixed pseudo-random order.
  19 books failed to download and were skipped.
- Debates: only speech paragraphs; speaker names, stage directions (`(Applaudissements ...)`), vote lists
  and tables of contents are skipped, and so is the AN annex of amendments. One Sénat issue
  (`SEN_20121024_058`) had a broken archive and was skipped.
- LEGI: only article versions with `ETAT = VIGUEUR`; HTML of the article body stripped. Articles are split
  into 20 buckets by article id (`id mod 20`); buckets 00-09 are counted so that the official texts are
  about as large as in German (all 20 buckets: 26,342,378 tokens from 195,460 articles).
- service-public.gouv.fr: `<Paragraphe>` and `<Titre>` text of the fiches `F*.xml`; a paragraph repeated
  within a fiche (conditional fiches repeat text per case) is counted once.

## Known limitations
- Corrected on 2026-10-21: the Wikipedia cleaning unescaped HTML entities only once, so `&nbsp;` and similar
  entities were counted as words. The list was not recounted; the forms `nbsp` (15,608), `thinsp` (24), `emsp` (21),
  `shy` (16), `ensp` (11) and `apos` (3) were removed with `tools/frequency/drop_forms.py` and the totals reduced
  (`STATS.md`, "Removed artefacts"). `count_wiki.py` now unescapes twice.
- Literature has 17th-19th century spellings (`oeuvre`/`œuvre`, `poëte`, `long-temps`); the per-source
  columns allow weighting.
- Légifrance often writes capitals without accents (`Etat` beside `État`); counts are kept as written.
- Wikipedia contains Belgian, Swiss, Canadian and African French without a marker; it is counted as French.
- Foreign words and names are counted as they are; the tail is noisy.
